# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a technical community where people can participate without harassment or intimidation. Be direct about ideas and considerate toward people. Assume good faith, make criticism specific, and correct mistakes openly.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, impersonation, publishing private information, and retaliation against a reporter.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the verified contact on the GitHub organization profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban an account. They will limit disclosure to people needed to assess and resolve the report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
